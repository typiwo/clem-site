— How it works

AI reads the label. Code computes the score.

The number isn’t an opinion. It’s a weighted formula over five dimensions, run on the label Clem just read. Scan the same product twice and you get the same score. So does everyone else.

Same food, same number.

What the code does

  • Computes the 0–100 score from five weighted dimensions
  • Grades each dimension A through F
  • Applies the penalties and score caps
  • Decides your green / yellow / red verdict from your profile

What the AI does

  • Reads the product label out of your photo
  • Writes Clem’s Take, the short editorial read
  • Nothing else. It never touches the math
— The formula

Five dimensions, each one graded.

Every scan shows all five as letter grades, so you can see exactly where a score came from. Nothing is hidden and nothing is weighted in secret.

How Clem’s 0–100 score is weighted
DimensionWeightWhat it measuresGrounded in
Ingredients 30% Fewer, more recognizable ingredients score higher; flagged ingredients pull it down. Machado et al., 2021
Processing 25% NOVA classification. Ultra-processed foods are linked to higher disease risk across 9.9M participants. Lane et al., 2024 · BMJ
Additives 20% Each additive rated by safety tier, with the reason spelled out. CSPI Chemical Cuisine
Nutrients to Limit 15% Sugar, sodium and saturated fat measured against public health thresholds. WHO 2015 / AHA
Nutrient Density 10% Credit for foods that actually nourish: protein, fiber, and nutrient density. ANDI nutrient-density framework

— The verdict

The number rates the food. The verdict is about you.

Tell Clem your diet, your allergens, and what you’d rather avoid. The score stays exactly the same. What changes is the call Clem makes on it.

Diets: Standard, Keto, Paleo, Mediterranean, Vegan, Vegetarian. Allergen flags: gluten, dairy, tree nuts. Avoidances: seed oils, added sugars, additives, ultra-processed foods.

Green · Great Choice

High-quality product that fits your dietary profile.

Yellow · Worth a Look

Doesn’t fully fit your goals: maybe a diet mismatch, like carbs on keto, or something on your watchlist.

Red · Not For You

Contains a flagged allergen, or conflicts with your core diet. A red verdict overrides a high score.
