Sugar and carb insights

Sugar and carbs, read off the label.

Scan any food with a nutrition label. Clem estimates how quickly its sugar and carbs tend to be taken in: fast, medium, slow or almost none. Then it shows what speeds it up and what slows it down, in grams. Built for anyone watching their sugar and carbs.

How it reads a label

Fast, medium, slow or almost none.

Per labeled serving, Clem weighs what speeds a food’s sugar and carbs up against what slows them down, and lands on one of four speeds. The card shows you both sides.

Fast

Taken in quickly

High in sugar or refined starch, with little to slow it down.

Medium

A moderate pace

Some things speed it up, some slow it down.

Slow

Taken in gradually

Fiber, protein or fat slow its sugar and carbs down.

Almost none

Very little to take in

Under 5 g of carbs per labeled serving once fiber is set aside.

What it reads

↑ Toward fast

  • Sugar per serving
  • Starchy carbs, more so when a refined starch leads the ingredients
  • A sweetener first, or near the top, of the ingredient list

↓ Toward slow

  • Fiber
  • Protein
  • Fat

Speed is not amount. A slow food can still carry a lot of carbs, so check the grams and the serving size too.

Good to know
  • Like the score, the rating comes from fixed rules over the Nutrition Facts and the ingredient list. AI reads the label; it doesn’t decide the rating.
  • Every rating is an estimate from the food’s label, which can be misread or out of date. It is not a measurement of you, and people respond to the same food differently.
  • It is general information, not medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat, prevent or manage any condition. Never use it to dose insulin or decide on medication.
  • Talk to your doctor or dietitian before changing your diet or medication.
  • Clem never asks about a health condition and doesn’t connect to meters or Apple Health. Turning the card on saves one fact to your account: that you turned it on, and when. How we handle it.
Getting started

How to turn it on.

Sugar and carb insights are optional and free. The card only reads the food’s label, nothing about you.

01

Say yes

When Clem asks “Want sugar and carb insights?” during setup, tap yes. Or turn it on any time in Settings.

02

Scan a label

Point the camera at the back of the pack. Every food with a nutrition label gets the card.

03

Read both sides

The speed, what speeds it up, what slows it down, and the serving it’s based on. Tap “How we read this” for the rules.

How it works

AI reads the label, and code does the math.

The sugar and carbs speed and the score both come from fixed rules run on the label Clem just read. Scan the same product twice and you get the same answer.

Same food, same answer.

What the code does

  • Rates sugar and carbs fast, medium, slow or almost none
  • Shows what speeds them up and what slows them down, in grams
  • Computes the 0–100 score from five weighted dimensions
  • Decides your green / yellow / red verdict from your profile

What the AI does

  • Reads the product label out of your photo
  • Writes Clem’s Take, the short editorial read
  • That’s all. It doesn’t calculate anything
The score

Every food gets an honest score, too.

Next to the sugar and carbs card, every scan scores the food 0–100 on five research-backed dimensions. All five show up as letter grades, so you can see where a score came from.

How Clem’s 0–100 score is weighted
DimensionWeightWhat it measuresGrounded in
Ingredients 30% Fewer, more recognizable ingredients score higher; flagged ingredients pull it down. Machado et al., 2021
Processing 25% NOVA classification. Ultra-processed foods are linked to higher disease risk across 9.9M participants. Lane et al., 2024 · BMJ
Additives 20% Each additive rated by safety tier, with the reason spelled out. CSPI Chemical Cuisine
Nutrients to Limit 15% Sugar, sodium and saturated fat measured against public health thresholds. WHO 2015 / AHA
Nutrient Density 10% Credit for foods that actually nourish: protein, fiber, and nutrient density. ANDI nutrient-density framework

The verdict

The number rates the food. The verdict is about you.

Tell Clem your diet, your allergens, and what you’d rather avoid. The score doesn’t change. Clem’s call on whether it suits you does.

Diets: Standard, Keto, Paleo, Mediterranean, Vegan, Vegetarian. Allergen flags: gluten, dairy, tree nuts. Avoidances: seed oils, added sugars, additives, ultra-processed foods.

Green · Great Choice

High-quality product that fits your dietary profile.

Yellow · Worth a Look

Doesn’t fully fit your goals: maybe a diet mismatch, like carbs on keto, or something on your watchlist.

Red · Not For You

Contains a flagged allergen, or conflicts with your core diet. A red verdict overrides a high score.
