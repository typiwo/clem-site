# Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 10, 2026
**Last updated:** November 10, 2026

This policy explains how Clemapp, Inc. ("**Clem**," "**we**," "**us**") collects, uses, and shares **consumer health data**, as that term is used in the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada Senate Bill 370, Connecticut's consumer health data law, and similar state laws. It supplements our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/), which covers everything else Clem collects. If the two differ on consumer health data, this policy controls.

## 1. When Clem handles consumer health data

Most people who use Clem share no health data with us. Clem scores grocery products; it does not ask about your health, and it never asks whether you have diabetes or any other condition.

The exception is the optional **sugar and carbs card**. Anyone can turn it on, either when Clem asks "Want sugar and carb insights?" during setup or later in **Settings**. The card estimates, from a food's label alone, how quickly its sugar and carbs tend to be taken in. Because the card is offered to people watching their sugar and carbs, including people living with type 2 diabetes, the fact that you turned it on could suggest something about your health. We therefore treat that fact, and how you use the card, as consumer health data.

Clem never collects your blood sugar readings, glucose monitor data, diagnoses, medications, insulin doses, or Apple Health (HealthKit) data. The card is calculated from the product's nutrition label, not from anything about you.

## 2. What we collect

If you turn the card on, we collect:

- **That you turned it on, and when,** and, if you later turn it off, that you did and when.
- **Your member number:** the order in which you first turned the card on (for example, #128). We show it to you once when you turn the card on. It never changes and is never reused.
- **How you use the card:** for example, when the card is shown on a scan (with the food's rating and whether the rating was estimated), when you open "How we read this," where you turned the card on or off (setup, Settings, or a one-time check), and your answer to the setup question.
- **Session replays** of the screens that show the card or the setup question, as described in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/). Text you type is masked.
- **If you contact us:** the emails you send our team, including anything you choose to tell us.
- **From the earlier invite-only program (before November 10, 2026):** if you were a member, we still hold your invite code, when and how you joined, your member number, and any survey answers or feedback note you gave. We will delete these records when we retire that program's database tables, and will update this policy when we do.

## 3. Where it comes from

Directly from you when you answer the setup question, use the Settings switch, use the card, or contact us.

## 4. Why we use it

Only to:

- show you the card on your scans;
- count how the card is used and improve it, and find and fix usability problems;
- reply to you.

We do not use consumer health data for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not send it to the AI that analyzes your food, and we do not use it to make decisions about you.

## 5. Who we share it with

Only with service providers that process it for us under contract, for the purposes in Section 4:

- **Supabase** (our database): whether the card is on, and your account.
- **PostHog** (product analytics and session replay): the card events and replays described in Section 2.
- **Google Workspace** (our team's email): emails you send us.

We do not share consumer health data with advertisers, data brokers, or the AI that analyzes your food. Clem has no advertising or attribution software in the app. We will disclose consumer health data to others only if the law requires it, and we never sell it.

## 6. Your consent

We collect consumer health data only with your consent. You give it by tapping **YES, TURN IT ON** on the setup question, **TURN IT ON** in Settings, or **KEEP IT ON** on the one-time check shown to people who turned the card on before this policy. Each of those screens says, before you tap, what we store, why, who stores it, that it is never shared with advertisers or sold, and how to turn it off, and links to this policy.

You can withdraw consent at any time by turning the card off in **Settings**. That stops the card and stops further collection of card events. The rest of Clem keeps working.

## 7. Your rights

You can:

- **See** the consumer health data we hold about you and the list of companies we share it with;
- **Delete** it, by deleting your account in **Settings → Delete Account**, or by asking us to remove only your card data;
- **Withdraw your consent**, by turning the card off in Settings.

To exercise any of these, email **privacy@clemapp.com** from the email address on your Clem account, with "Health data request" in the subject line. We will respond within 45 days. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying with "Health data appeal" in the subject line; we will answer the appeal in writing within 45 days. If the appeal is denied, you may contact your state attorney general (in Washington, at [atg.wa.gov](https://www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint)).

Deleting your account deletes your card data from our live database immediately, and from backups within 30 days. To also remove your analytics profile and its card events and replays from PostHog, email us as above and we will delete them within 45 days.

## 8. Security

Consumer health data gets the same protections as the rest of your data: encryption in transit and at rest, and database rules that let only you read your own records. Access by our team is limited to the people who build and support the card.

## 9. Where to find this policy

In the app (**Settings**, next to the sugar and carbs switch, and on every screen that asks you to turn the card on), on our App Store page, and at the bottom of clemapp.com.

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell people who have the card on, in the app, before any new collection or sharing begins.
